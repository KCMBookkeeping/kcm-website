Frequently asked questions

Still have questions? Reach out anytime — we're happy to talk through specifics before you commit.

What's included in a monthly engagement?

Every engagement includes transaction reconciliation and a monthly close, at minimum. From there, we scope in exactly what your business needs — bill pay, invoicing, cash flow reporting, sales tax filing, payroll coordination, or any combination — and quote accordingly.

How does onboarding work?

We start with a quick call to understand your current books and accounts. If you need catch-up bookkeeping or a QuickBooks Online migration first, we handle that before your regular monthly cycle begins.

Is my financial data secure?

Yes — we work inside QuickBooks Online's secure platform rather than passing spreadsheets or files back and forth over email, and access is limited to the two of us.

Do you work with businesses nationwide?

Yes — we're a fully remote practice, so we work with small businesses anywhere in the U.S. Everything runs through QuickBooks Online and scheduled calls, so location never gets in the way.
